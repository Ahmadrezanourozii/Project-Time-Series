\section{Conclusion}

We classified Parkinson's disease from vertical ground reaction force
recordings with a Mamba state-space model on five subject-disjoint folds. On
both feet it reaches $75 \pm 4$\,\% accuracy, $0.75$ weighted F1 and $0.85$ AUC
at subject level: ahead of the SVM-RBF, 1D-ResNet and LSTM baselines run
through the same evaluation code, and level with a tuned Random Forest. The
gain came from the input representation, not the architecture.

The methodological finding may be the more useful one. A window-wise split
raises Random Forest accuracy from 73\,\% to 98\,\% with no other change to
features, models or metrics: when one recording yields more than a hundred
correlated windows, an accuracy quoted for this database cannot be interpreted
until the split unit is stated. We chose the final configuration on validation
AUC for the same reason. Every model plateaued in the same band, pointing at
the cohort rather than the method: pooling the dual-task recordings and other
public VGRF cohorts is the obvious next step.
